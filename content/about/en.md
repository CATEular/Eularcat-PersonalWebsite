## About me

My primary research direction is **high-current DC-DC Buck**. Synchronous DC-DC Buck is one step in my learning, with multi-phase Buck and related topics also part of my ongoing study.

I’m exploring practical ways AI agents can participate in circuit-design workflows, including EDA automation, simulation, result analysis and iterative optimization.

## This site

This is where I document what I learn, read, build and test.

It is a place for my own review, and hopefully a useful resource for students learning similar topics. Content grows with actual study and engineering practice.

## Interests

- Power-management ICs (PMIC)
- High-current DC-DC Buck
- Multi-phase DC-DC Buck
- AI-assisted EDA
- EDA automation and tools
